While I do what I can to offer my recommendation and answer any questions you have, the final choice is yours to make. I want to take a few moments to explain more about some of the tooth replacement options I provide to my patients. Read on to learn more.

## Tooth Replacement Options

There are a number of reasons you may experience tooth loss at some point in your life, such as severe decay, trauma to the mouth, a medical condition, or an illness. No matter the reason for losing a tooth, I encourage my patients to replace any missing teeth as soon as possible. I believe in choosing an option that is aesthetically pleasing while restoring the function of your tooth but also promotes life-long health. I may recommend a treatment such as:

Dental Implants

This option is typically what I recommend first to patients. [This treatment](/service/dental-implants/) uses a multi-step process that includes an artificial tooth root being surgically implanted into the jawbone. After a few more steps, the process is complete when a tooth structure, such as a crown, is placed on top. Implants can also be used to support bridges.

Dentures and Partials

You might want full dentures if you are missing all of your teeth. I offer a number of [full dentures](/service/dentures/) based on your needs. You might need a partial denture if you are missing several teeth in different parts of your mouth or you are missing teeth on both sides of the arch.
