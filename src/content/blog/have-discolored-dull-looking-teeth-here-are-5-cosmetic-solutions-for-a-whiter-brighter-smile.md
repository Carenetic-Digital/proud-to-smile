We all want to flash a bright, white smile, but everyday choices like coffee and red wine can stain your enamel and make your teeth look dull and dingy. Whether it’s our food and beverage selections or just the natural aging process, teeth can lose their sparkle over time.

With a full menu of [cosmetic dental solutions](/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry/) at [Proud to Smile Dentistry](/about/) in Bernardsville, New Jersey, [Patricia Swaintek-Lamb,DMD](/doctors/patricia-swaintek-lamb-dmd/), and [our expert team](/doctors/) are here to help you restore your pearly whites in no time flat.

## 1. Professional teeth whitening

One of the quickest and most popular ways to achieve a brighter smile is through professional [teeth whitening](/service/teeth-whitening/). This quick in-office procedure provides immediate results that you’ll notice as soon as the treatment is done.

With professional whitening, we use stronger, more concentrated whitening agents than those found in over-the-counter whitening products. These products are more effective and can offer immediate results.

If you’re in a rush to brighten your smile, this option is perfect since it works quickly and yields noticeable improvements with just one visit.

## 2. At-home whitening options

If you’d prefer to take things slow or handle the whitening process in the comfort of your home, we offer a clinical-grade option for at-home use, as well.

Dr. Swaintek-Lamb can provide custom whitening trays designed to fit your teeth perfectly. With these trays, you apply a whitening gel at your convenience, allowing you to work on your smile in a more relaxed, flexible manner.

Since these trays are custom-made, they provide a better fit and offer more consistent results than store-bought options. The process may take a little longer, but many people appreciate the gradual improvement they see over time. Plus, it’s ideal for anyone who wants the benefits of professional treatment with the comfort of doing it on their own schedule.

## 3. Dental veneers

Teeth whitening can’t always tackle stubborn stains or repair damage, and that’s where dental veneers shine. [Veneers](/service/veneers/) are thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth, instantly creating a brighter, more uniform smile.

The custom-made restorations cover discoloration, chips, and cracks while improving tooth shape and symmetry. Veneers deliver immediate results and, with proper care, can last for many years before they need to be replaced. If you want a long-term solution for both color and cosmetic imperfections, veneers are an excellent choice.

## 4. Dental bonding

For smaller areas of discoloration or minor imperfections, dental bonding is an excellent alternative. The procedure involves applying a tooth-colored resin to the surface of the affected tooth. The resin is shaped to match the natural contour of your tooth and hardened with a special light.

The best part about dental bonding is that it’s one of the most cost-effective options for improving the appearance of your teeth. The process is quick and usually takes about 30 minutes.

Plus, there’s little to no preparation involved, making it an excellent option for those who want fast, affordable results without committing to more invasive treatments.

## 5. At-home and professional oral care

No cosmetic treatment can replace the value of a solid oral care routine. Maintaining a good routine ensures your teeth stay in top condition and keeps stains from settling in. Be sure to brush twice a day, floss at least once a day, and use a whitening toothpaste to help reduce plaque buildup and surface stains.

It’s also a good idea to minimize your intake of enamel-staining foods and drinks, like coffee, tea, red wine, berries, and chocolate — especially after a whitening treatment. And don’t forget to visit your dentist regularly for [cleanings](/service/cleaning/) to clear away enamel-yellowing plaque and tartar and keep your teeth and gums healthy.
