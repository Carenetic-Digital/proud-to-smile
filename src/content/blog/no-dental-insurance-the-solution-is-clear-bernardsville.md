## How Does Our Membership Plan Work?

Dr. Patti Swaintek-Lamb has been taking care of patients here in Bernardsville for almost 30 years and her goal has always been to provide the best dental treatment in the area. We believe that good dental health is an investment and in our quest to make sure all patients have the care the need, we are now offering membership plans -- this is a great option for our uninsured patients.

Our Adult Plan includes two dental cleanings, two dental exams, and one emergency exam per year plus 10%-20% off other treatments, like fillings, crowns, and more. This plan is just $37/month.

Our Child Plan includes two dental cleanings, two dental exams, one emergency exam, and one fluoride treatment per year plus 10%-20% off other treatments, like fillings, crowns, and more. This plan is just $31/month.

## How Can You Enroll?

If you're interested in learning more or would like to sign up, you can contact our front desk and enroll over the phone. You can also sign up online at kleer.com -- simply enter our office code FR9N to get started.

Enrollment takes less than 5 minutes and your membership plan lasts for 12 months from the date you join. Re-enrollment is also very easy, but as always we are happy to help you every step of the way.
