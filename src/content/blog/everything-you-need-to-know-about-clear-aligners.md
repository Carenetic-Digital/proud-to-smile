## Why tooth and bite alignment matters

It may seem that teeth straightening is a purely aesthetic procedure, but having good alignment is essential to dental health. Let’s get into the reasons why proper tooth alignment matters.

When teeth aren’t aligned properly, they create crevices, corners, and pockets that can be hard to keep clean. This allows [bacteria](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/crooked-teeth-misaligned-bites#1) to take hold and cause decay, leading to the erosion of your dental structures. Your risk for bad breath, minor cavities, major decay, gum infection, and other dental issues increases.

Misaligned teeth affect your bite and result in [uneven wear](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/crooked-teeth) across your bite surfaces. This leads to a higher risk of injury to your gums and [gum disease](https://fightgumdisease.com/about/), as well as an increased risk of tooth damage such as cracking, breaking, chipping, and tooth loss.

Temporomandibular disorder, referred to as [TMJ or TMD](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/tmj/symptoms-causes/syc-20350941), can also develop when your teeth don’t line up as they should. This condition can cause pain and trouble chewing and eating. Crooked bites can lead to teeth clenching or grinding, and may lead to a higher risk of having a dental emergency.

## All about Invisalign clear aligners

Most people know that Invisalign clear aligners are removable and free from metal, a key feature that makes Invisalign so easy to fit into your lifestyle. But there are some other great benefits of Invisalign that you probably didn’t know.

Invisalign is made from [SmartTrack®](https://www.invisalign.com/invisalign-digital-experience) material, a stiff yet highly-elastic material that makes your clear retainer comfortable while still applying a gentle but constant force on your teeth to move them to the right location. This technology helps straighten your teeth 50% faster than traditional braces.

Invisalign also provides [SmartForce®](https://www.invisalign.com/invisalign-digital-experience) attachments for troublesome teeth, eliminating the need for painful and unsightly rubber bands. These minuscule attachments help the aligners put the right amount of pressure on troublesome teeth to help straighten them out.

The [iTero Element®](https://www.invisalign.com/invisalign-digital-experience) scanner from Invisalign allows your Proud to Smile Dentistry provider to create instant 3D images of your teeth, ensuring a precise fit for the best results. This technology, combined with data from over [7 million](https://www.invisalign.com/frequently-asked-questions) Invisalign users improves the teeth straightening algorithm to help you attain straighter teeth and a perfect smile as quickly and comfortably as possible.
