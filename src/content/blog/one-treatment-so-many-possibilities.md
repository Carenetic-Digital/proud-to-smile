### What are Porcelain Veneers?

If you are looking to improve your smile and have a celebrity worthy smile, we can help! [Porcelain veneers](/service/veneers/) are thin shells of porcelain that are bonded to the front surface of the teeth. Many patients love veneers because they blend in seamlessly with your natural teeth. Veneers are perfect for anyone with:

- Severe discoloration
- Crooked or uneven teeth
- Teeth that are too small
- Gaps between teeth
- Chipped teeth

### Benefits of Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are typically used to improve the front, visible teeth. They can be used to improve multiple teeth or a single tooth. Patients choose porcelain veneers over other options because they resist staining and are not susceptible to tooth decay. The durability of the porcelain allows veneers to appear perfect for years. The maintenance of veneers is the same as your natural teeth, only needing to be brushed and flossed daily. We do recommend using a soft-bristled toothbrush with gentle strokes to avoid damaging the veneers.

### A Permanent Fix

It is important to understand that veneers are a permanent change to your teeth. The tooth preparation needed makes the process irreversible. To allow a precise fit the natural teeth are often prepped or ground down before the porcelain veneer is applied.
