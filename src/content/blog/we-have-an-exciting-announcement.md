## Aesthetic Dentistry of Bernardsville is becoming Proud To Smile Dentistry!

It’s an exciting time for me and my team, and we are thrilled to share it with all of our valued patients. The practice is in the final stages of a big expansion. The new office will be ready in the fall, which is right around the corner. And with this new office, comes a new look and a new name — Proud To Smile Dentistry.

I chose Proud To Smile because that is and always has been my goal — to make every patient proud to smile. Dentistry is more than my profession, it’s my passion. I absolutely love making people healthier and happier every day. My dream has always been to have a practice that provides gentle, compassionate, top-quality dental care.

At this point, Aesthetic Dentistry of Bernardsville has been a community fixture for ten years now, and it is time for a new phase, Proud To Smile, which will be bigger and even better, but with the same trusted people you are used to seeing, me — Dr. Patti — and my amazing team. We will always maintain our same core values, because that’s who we are. Only now, we’ll be providing our signature excellent service under a new name that better embodies our philosophy of “Helping our patients be proud to smile and successful in sleep.”

Yours in health,

Patti Swaintek-Lamb, DMD
