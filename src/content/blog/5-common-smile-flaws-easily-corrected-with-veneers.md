Are you holding back your smile because of imperfections that make you self-conscious? At [Proud to Smile Dentistry](/about/) in Bernardsville, New Jersey, we understand that a confident smile can light up your face and boost your self-confidence.

That’s why we offer [veneers](/service/veneers/) — a simple solution that enhances your smile while correcting a number of common cosmetic issues. Crafted from porcelain or resin, these [custom-made shells](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/guide/veneers#1) fit perfectly over your teeth, providing a naturally flawless look that addresses your dental concerns effectively.

From the initial consultation to the final application, our skilled dentist, [Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb](/doctors/patricia-swaintek-lamb-dmd/), and our entire [care team](/doctors/) ensure your veneers complement your natural teeth for a flawless finish. Read on to discover how veneers can turn your dental worries into a radiant smile you’ll love sharing.

## 1. Closing dental gaps

It's not uncommon for teeth to shift over the years, creating unwanted gaps that may affect your smile. Whether these spaces have formed due to natural changes or past orthodontic treatments, veneers offer a quick and aesthetically pleasing solution.

Unlike more time-intensive options, like braces, veneers can [bridge any dental gaps](https://www.gotoapro.org/veneers/) you have quickly and beautifully, enhancing your natural smile without the extended treatment time.

## 2. Repairing chips and cracks

Accidents happen, and they can leave behind chips and cracks that tarnish your smile. These imperfections can occur despite excellent oral hygiene and routine dental visits, often resulting from simple mishaps like biting into hard food.

Veneers are a great way to conceal damage like this quickly and effectively. In fact, you’ll have the integrity and appearance of your smile completely restored after just a few visits.

## 3. Overcoming deep discoloration

If you’re dissatisfied with the shade of your teeth but have been told traditional [whitening treatments](/service/teeth-whitening/) can't correct your deeper, more stubborn stains, veneers provide an effective solution.

Veneers are particularly useful for [intrinsic stains](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/10958-tooth-discoloration), or dental stains that lie beneath the surface of your teeth. With veneers, you’ll enjoy a uniformly brighter smile that is resistant to discoloration.

## 4. Balancing uneven teeth

Some people have teeth that are noticeably uneven in size or shape, which can disrupt the harmony of their smile. This is often more pronounced in individuals who experience [bruxism](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/health/conditions-and-diseases/bruxism), or teeth grinding.

If you’ve noticed your teeth are uneven, our team can custom craft veneers to adjust the appearance of these uneven teeth. You’ll leave with a balanced, more uniform look that enhances your overall smile.

## 5. Concealing unsightly dental work

Visible dental repairs, like large fillings or prominent [crowns](/service/crowns/), can detract from the natural beauty of your smile. At Proud to Smile Dentistry, we can use veneers to cover and camouflage poorly aging dental work, helping ensure your smile is both healthy and attractive.

Veneers aren’t just an effective way to improve your smile’s appearance, but they’re also [simple to maintain](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/cosmetic-dentistry/veneers/what-are-veneers-and-how-should-you-care-for-them-0814). This means you can keep unsightly old dental work concealed and preserve your bright new smile for years to come.
