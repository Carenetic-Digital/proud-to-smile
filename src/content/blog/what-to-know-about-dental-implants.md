### What are Dental Implants?

[Dental implants](/service/dental-implants/) are the best alternative to natural teeth because they look and function much like your own teeth. A dental implant is an artificial tooth root that is surgically implanted into the jawbone, mimicking the function of a natural tooth root. Over time, this prosthesis fuses with the bone to create a permanent solution. The process is often finished with a dental crown. Implants can be used to replace one tooth, multiple teeth or an entire arch of teeth.

### Benefits of Dental Implants

When patients are missing a tooth or multiple teeth, it can affect everyday life. It can be difficult for patients to chew and enjoy their favorite foods. Patients may also experience difficulty speaking properly. Dental implants restore the complete function of the teeth, allowing patients to speak and chew normally. Patients are able to enjoy foods that they may not have been able to eat for years. Implants also help the jawbone by providing the stimulation needed to ensure optimal bone health. Other benefits include:

- Improved self-esteem
- Permanent solution
- Blends in with natural teeth
- Long-lasting
