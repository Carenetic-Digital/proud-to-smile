## Understanding your assessment and treatment plan

Your smile makeover is a highly personalized treatment designed to improve the appearance and health of your teeth. It can include a variety of [cosmetic and restorative dental procedures](/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry/), each selected to address your unique needs and goals.

Dr. Swaintek-Lamb considers factors such as your facial structure, teeth alignment, and overall dental health to ensure the best results. Here are some treatments you might expect:

- Gum contouring to reshape your gum line and create a more balanced smile
- [Veneers](/service/veneers/) to conceal cracks, chips, discoloration, gaps, and other imperfections
- [Clear aligners](/service/invisalign/) to straighten your teeth without the need for traditional braces
- Professional [teeth whitening](/service/teeth-whitening/) to brighten your smile by several shades
- [Dental crowns](/service/crowns/) or bridges to fully restore severely damaged or missing teeth
- Dental bonding using resin to fill gaps, repair chips, and improve tooth shape

We assess your goals, oral health, and appearance of your smile to develop a comprehensive treatment plan, which includes the recommended procedures and the expected timeline for your makeover.

## What you can expect from the smile makeover procedures

Each treatment in your smile makeover has its own process and recovery time. The ones included in your plan determine what you can expect from your process. Dr. Swaintek-Lamb explains what you can expect for each procedure, but here’s a brief overview for some of the most common treatments:

### Gum contouring

This minimally invasive procedure involves using a [laser to reshape](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/25208-gum-contouring) your gums. Recovery is usually quick, with minor discomfort that can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

### Porcelain veneers

The veneer process typically requires three visits: At the first visit, we discuss the changes you'd like to make to your smile and take impressions. On your second visit, we will prep your teeth and apply temporary veneers. Three weeks later, you will return for your final visit where we bond the custom-made veneers to your teeth"

### Clear aligners

[Clear aligners](https://aaoinfo.org/treatments/aligners/) like Invisalign® are custom-made to fit your teeth. You’ll wear each set of aligners for about two weeks before moving on to the next set, gradually straightening your teeth over a period of time determined by your unique needs.

### Teeth whitening

Professional teeth whitening with [Opalescence™](https://www.opalescence.com) is a quick and painless cosmetic dental treatment. You can expect noticeable results after just one session, with further improvement after subsequent treatments, if desired.

### Crowns and bridges

Getting crowns or bridges also usually requires two visits. The first visit includes preparing your natural teeth and taking impressions, while the second involves fitting and placing your final restorations.

### Dental bonding

This [quick procedure](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health/teeth-bonding) takes only one visit for Dr. Swaintek-Lamb to apply the resin, shape it to fit your tooth, and harden it with a special light. The bonded tooth is then polished to match your natural teeth.
