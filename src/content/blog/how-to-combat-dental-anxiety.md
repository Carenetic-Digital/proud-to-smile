Dental anxiety is more common than you think: Every year, fear of the dentist's chair prevents millions of people from getting the care their teeth and gums need to thrive. But putting off dental check-ups can trigger a chain reaction of health issues later.

## What causes dental anxiety?

Fear of dental visits can grasp us from many angles — for some, it’s the looming dread of pain, while for others, it’s the memory of difficult past experiences or even the faint whiff of antiseptic that fills the air. If the mere thought of sitting in a dentist’s chair triggers feelings of nervousness or panic for you, understanding what causes your anxiety is the first step toward addressing it.

## Let us know about your fears

One of the most important things you can do to combat dental anxiety is to communicate your fears with us. We have experience with anxious patients and can take steps to make your visit more comfortable.

Before your appointment, just let us know what makes you anxious. We can explain procedures in detail and offer calming techniques to help ease your nerves.

## Relaxation strategies to help with dental anxiety

There are several relaxation techniques that can make a big difference in how you feel during a dental visit. Deep breathing exercises, for instance, can help reduce tension and promote a sense of calm. Practice slow, deep breaths before and during your appointment.

Another effective technique is progressive muscle relaxation, which involves tensing and then slowly relaxing each muscle group in your body. These techniques can help distract your mind and keep you relaxed in the dentist’s chair.

## Distraction techniques at the dentist

Stress can make a trip to the dentist feel like a nightmare, but cleverly using distractions can help calm those nerves. Mind-wandering can be a blessing in disguise during dental procedures. Bringing your own headphones and listening to your favorite calming music or podcast is another great way to keep anxiety at bay.

## Why is regular dental care important?

Overcoming dental anxiety is crucial because regular dental care is vital for maintaining good oral health and your overall well-being. Skipping dental visits can lead to more complicated procedures later on, which will probably only serve to reinforce your anxiety.

By finding ways to manage your fears, you can keep your teeth and gums healthy and avoid more invasive treatments in the future.

Taking control of your dental anxiety is possible with the right approach. Understanding the root of your fears, communicating openly so we can help you, and exploring relaxation options can make dental visits a more positive experience.
