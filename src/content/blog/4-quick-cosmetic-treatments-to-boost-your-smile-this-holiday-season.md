A bright, confident smile is one of the best accessories for holiday photos, parties, and family gatherings. Whether you want a subtle boost or a dramatic change, there are fast, effective [cosmetic dental treatments](/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry/) that can help you look and feel your best in time for the festivities.

At [Proud to Smile Dentistry](/about/) in Bernardsville, New Jersey, [Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb](/doctors/patricia-swaintek-lamb-dmd/) and [our expert team](/doctors/) help patients refresh their smiles quickly and comfortably before the busy season.

## 1. Professional teeth cleaning

A professional [cleaning](/service/cleaning/) is one of the fastest ways to refresh your smile. Even if you brush and floss daily, plaque and tartar can build up and dull your teeth.

A cleaning removes plaque buildup, polishes enamel, and leaves your teeth looking brighter and feeling smoother. It also supports gum health, giving you a fresher, healthier smile for holiday photos and events.

## 2. Teeth whitening

If you want a more noticeable boost, professional whitening is a great option. Our in-office [whitening treatments](/service/teeth-whitening/) can lift stubborn stains and brighten your smile several shades in just one visit.

Unlike over-the-counter products, professional whitening is customized for comfort and effectiveness. It’s a simple, fast way to feel photo-ready for holiday celebrations and special moments.

## 3. Porcelain veneers

For chips, gaps, discoloration, or uneven enamel, [porcelain veneers](/service/veneers/) offer a stunning smile transformation in a short timeline. These thin porcelain shells bond to the front of your teeth to create a smooth, symmetrical, natural-looking smile.

While treatment requires a couple of visits, many people are surprised by how quickly they see results. Veneers are long-lasting and deliver dramatic cosmetic improvements without extensive dental work.

## 4. Botox for facial aesthetics

Cosmetic dentistry extends beyond your teeth. [Botox](/service/botox/) treatments can relax facial muscles, soften lines, and create a smoother, more youthful look around your smile. It can also help reduce the appearance of a gummy smile for more balanced proportions.

Botox treatment typically takes just minutes, and results appear within a few days, lasting throughout the holiday season and beyond.

## Smile bright this holiday season

Your smile plays a big part in how confident you feel during the holidays. These fast cosmetic treatments can help you look polished, refreshed, and ready for every photo and celebration.
