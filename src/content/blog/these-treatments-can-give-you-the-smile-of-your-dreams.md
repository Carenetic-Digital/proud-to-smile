## Why Choose Cosmetic Dentistry?

Over the past many years, there have been a number of advancements in the way dentistry is practiced as well as dental technology that have increased the number of treatments available to patients. Today, there are numerous cosmetic dentistry options available that can bring about any changes you would like to see to your smile. [Cosmetic dentistry](/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry/) can improve imperfections such as:

- Chipped or broken teeth
- Severely stained teeth
- Teeth that have gaps or overcrowding
- Cracked teeth
- Missing teeth
- Teeth that are misshapen or jagged

## Cosmetic Dentistry Treatments We Offer

We offer a number of treatments that can be used to enhance the appearance of your smile. Following an examination, we may recommend a treatment such as:

Teeth Whitening

If you are unhappy with the color of your teeth, this is a solution that is a simple and quick fix. [Teeth whitening](/service/teeth-whitening/) is one of the most popular cosmetic treatments completed in our office. It is used to remove severe staining.

Porcelain Veneers

[This option](/service/veneers/) is used to improve the appearance of the front teeth. Tooth-colored, wafer-thin shells are placed on the front of the teeth to improve a number of imperfections or damage to the teeth.

Dental Crowns

If the tooth structure is damaged or decayed beyond repair, a dental crown is a wonderful solution. A [tooth-colored cap](/service/crowns/) is placed over the damaged tooth creating a beautiful, natural appearance.

Dental Bonding

[This option](/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry/) uses tooth-colored resin to improve imperfections in a tooth. It can be used to repair damage such as chips or cracks.
