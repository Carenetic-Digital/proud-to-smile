## Understanding gum disease

Gum disease is an infection of the tissues that hold your teeth in place. It’s typically caused by poor oral hygiene that allows plaque — a sticky film of bacteria — to build up on the teeth and harden.

[Gingivitis](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/gingivitis/symptoms-causes/syc-20354453) is the earliest stage of gum disease, characterized by red, swollen gums that may bleed easily during brushing or flossing. Gingivitis is often painless and can be reversed with good oral hygiene and professional cleanings.

When not managed, gingivitis can become [periodontitis](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/242321.php), a more advanced and serious form of gum disease that causes your gums to pull away from your teeth, forming pockets that can become infected.

Over time, the bones and connective tissue that support your teeth can be destroyed. This can lead to tooth loss and other oral health issues.

Common [symptoms of gum disease](https://www.perio.org/for-patients/gum-disease-information/) include persistent bad breath, red or swollen gums, tender or bleeding gums, painful chewing, and loose teeth. Addressing these symptoms early is crucial to prevent further complications.

## What is Perio Protect?

Perio Protect is a comprehensive treatment approach for managing and preventing gum disease.

It involves using custom-fit dental trays, called [Perio Trays™](https://www.perioprotect.com/faq/), that deliver medication directly to your gum line, targeting the bacteria that cause periodontal disease.

Here’s how Perio Protect works:

First, Dr. Swaintek-Lamb creates personalized trays based on impressions of your teeth. These trays are designed to fit snugly against your gums, ensuring the medication reaches the areas where it’s needed most.

The trays are filled with a hydrogen peroxide-based gel that helps to [reduce the bacterial load](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/adult-oral-care/hydrogen-peroxide-gums-and-teeth-treatments) in your gum pockets. You wear the trays for a few minutes each day as part of your regular oral hygiene routine.

The [Perio Protect system](https://www.perioprotect.com/how-it-works/) is clinically proven to reduce gum inflammation and bleeding, while improving the depth of gum pockets, leading to healthier gums and teeth.

The convenience and effectiveness of Perio Protect make it an excellent option for maintaining your gum health, especially for people who are more vulnerable to gum disease.

## The benefits of using Perio Protect

Perio Protect offers benefits over traditional periodontal treatments. Here’s why it might be the right choice for you:

### Noninvasive treatment

Unlike some dental procedures, Perio Protect is noninvasive, meaning there’s no need for surgery or uncomfortable dental work. This makes it an ideal option if you’re looking to avoid more intensive gum therapy treatments.

### Convenient and easy to use

The Perio Protect trays are easy to incorporate into your daily routine. Simply wear them for a few minutes each day, and you’re on your way to better gum health.

### Effective for all stages of gum disease

Whether you’re dealing with early-stage gingivitis or advanced-stage periodontitis, Perio Protect can help [manage and reduce](https://www.perioprotect.com/results/) the symptoms of gum disease.

### Reduces the need for antibiotics

By targeting the bacteria directly, Perio Protect reduces the need for [systemic antibiotics](https://www.webmd.com/oral-health/gum-disease-treatments), which can have adverse side effects and contribute to antibiotic resistance.

### Better overall health

This therapy not only improves your oral health but also contributes to your overall wellness by reducing the risk of systemic health issues related to gum disease, such as [heart and respiratory disease](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13814788.2024.2320120), pregnancy complications, Alzheimer’s, and [diabetes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9954907/pdf/diagnostics-13-00681.pdf).
