### What is Teeth Whitening?

If you are looking to improve your smile before your next event, [teeth whitening](/service/teeth-whitening/) is a great choice. This popular cosmetic dentistry treatment improves and changes the whiteness of your teeth. Teeth whitening is completed in our office and can help improve discoloration and staining caused by:

- Red wine
- Cola
- Tobacco products
- Antibiotics
- Aging
- Coffee and tea

### Why Choose Professional Teeth Whitening?

When walking through most stores, you will see a variety of over-the-counter whitening treatments. It may make you wonder which is better, professional whitening or an over-the counter whitening system. Professional whitening is always the better choice. Not only is professional whitening customized to fit your needs, but it is also designed to provide immediate results. Over-the-counter products can damage your teeth, and many of these products don’t provide lasting results, if any results at all. The professional teeth whitening we use in our office can provide results for up to three years.
